# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary:** Recruiters, hiring managers, potential co-founders, partner organizations, conference
organizers — evaluating credibility and fit from a professional context (desktop, follow-up
after a handshake or LinkedIn). Their emotional state: wanting to be impressed, looking for
signal over noise.

**Secondary:** Peers and mentors in the startup, MUN, and academic community — familiar with
who Chin Wei is, coming to the site for depth or to share it with someone else.

The primary visitor gives you 60–90 seconds before deciding whether to contact or move on.
Every design choice serves that window.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand site for Chin Wei Ling — student at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia, TEDxUKM
lead organizer, entrepreneur, robotics leader, MUN chair, and 2× startup founder. The site has
three jobs:

1. **Credibility signal** — prove leadership depth without the visitor having to ask follow-up
   questions. The achievement record is extraordinary; the design should let it land, not bury
   it in visual noise.
2. **First impression** — leave a strong, memorable mark that a recruiter or partner thinks
   about after the tab is closed. Success = "I need to contact this person."
3. **Publishing platform** — a personal blog built into the site for long-form writing, not
   outsourced to Substack or Medium.

This is not a résumé. It is a portrait.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** Composed. Purposeful. Formidable.

Voice: first-person, direct, no filler. The site speaks like someone who has led rooms, not
performed for them. Confident without braggadocio. Dense achievements made to feel earned, not
listed. The tagline says it all — "Leader, Builder, Founder." — three nouns, no adjectives.

Aesthetic chosen by owner: **cinematic dark editorial** — deep blacks, large commanding type,
dramatic breathing room between sections. Dark is the natural state; light mode is a clean,
authoritative inversion, not an afterthought.

## Anti-references

- **Resume website** — list-dump, template-heavy, corporate blues, zero personality. Reads
  like a PDF export. The opposite of a portrait.
- **SaaS landing page** — metric-hero ("10,000+ users"), testimonial grids, gradient CTA
  buttons. Treats a person as a product in the wrong sense.
- **Social media bio** — influencer-adjacent, follower counts over substance. Fun but not
  authoritative enough for the primary audience.
- **Try-hard portfolio** — every scroll triggers 12 animations, slow load, Awwwards-chasing.
  Motion as costume, not communication.

## Design Principles

1. **Authority through restraint** — The achievement list is extraordinarily dense. Let
   breathing room and pacing carry weight, not animation. Motion earns its place or it's cut.

2. **Dark is the identity, light is the guest** — Dark mode is the primary voice. Light mode
   is a genuine alternate that shares the same typographic authority and accent color, not
   a washed-out inversion.

3. **Depth over volume** — Don't surface-level list every role. The design should feel
   curated: foregrounded selections, not exhaustive catalogs. Give the timeline structure;
   give the blog a voice.

4. **Typography is the hero** — Without confirmed photography on hand, type carries the
   brand. Use it with conviction: scale, weight, contrast. No decoration to substitute for
   weak typographic choices.

5. **Every element earns its place** — No decorative patterns for "elegance," no hover
   animations for "polish." Each design decision must serve communication, not signal effort.

## Typography note

The CLAUDE.md spec names Fraunces (headings) and Inter (body). Both appear on the impeccable
reflex-reject list — heavily saturated across AI-generated brand surfaces in 2026. The owner
confirmed Fraunces as a default with an invitation to compare Playfair Display once running.
Surface alternative pairing options during DESIGN.md and at first visual review; don't lock in
without showing the owner at least one alternative. The owner's preference stands if confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large/bold text).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout — crossfade or instant transition fallback for
  every animated element. Owner confirmed this explicitly.
- Multilingual name and Malaysian/Malay content — ensure correct character rendering.
- No color-only information encoding; accent (#78aaff) used for emphasis, never as the sole
  carrier of meaning.
